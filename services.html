Truckload freight, kept simple.

We haul full truckloads and dry van freight for shippers and freight brokers. Here is what we do and what to send us.

Full truckload

You have a trailer's worth of freight. We send one truck for it. It goes from your dock to the receiver's dock without being mixed in with anyone else's freight.

That means fewer touches, fewer hand-offs, and a simpler answer when someone asks where it is.

Good for

Freight that fills most of a trailer, or freight you don't want handled at a terminal.

What we need from you

Pickup and delivery locations, dates, approximate weight, and what's on the load.

Request a quote

Dry van freight

Our trailers are enclosed 53-foot dry vans. They're a good fit for palletized, boxed, and general freight that doesn't need temperature control.

The trailers are clean, the tractors are newer, and the driver knows what time the appointment is.

Good for

Pallets, cartons, and packaged goods that need to stay dry and enclosed.

What we need from you

Pallet count, weight, and any dock or loading requirements. Dry freight only, no temperature control.

Request a quote

Dedicated and recurring lanes

Shipping the same lane over and over? Let's set up a regular schedule. You know when the truck is coming. We know what's on it.

Same lane, same schedule, same people to call.

Good for

Regular freight between the same origin and destination, weekly or more.

What we need from you

The lane, how often it ships, typical volume, and the days and times that work best.

Request a quote

Time-sensitive freight

Tight delivery window? Tell us before you book. We'll look at the miles, the appointment times, and the driver's hours, then give you an honest answer on whether we can make it.

If we can't, we'll say so up front. That's better for both of us.

Good for

Freight with a hard appointment, a short pickup window, or a deadline that can't slip.

What we need from you

The pickup and delivery appointment times, and how firm they are.

Request a quote

What to send for a quote

  • Pickup and delivery city and state
  • Pickup date and any appointment window
  • Approximate weight and pallet count
  • Commodity and any special handling
  • Your reference or PO number, if you have one

Need an update on a load?

For booked loads, customers and logistics partners can request current location and shipment-status updates directly from our operations team. Call or message us and we'll tell you where it is.

We don't have a public tracking portal.
