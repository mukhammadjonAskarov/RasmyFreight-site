Newer equipment. Reliable service.

Rasmy Freight is a truckload carrier based in Bloomington, Minnesota.

Who we are.

Rasmy Freight LLC moves truckload and dry van freight for shippers and freight brokers.

We run newer trucks and trailers. We show up when we say we will. And when you need to know where a load is, you can call or message us and get an answer from a person, not a login screen.

From pickup to delivery, we stay on top of the load.

  • On time.

    Pickup and delivery appointments are the job. We plan the run around them.

  • Easy to reach.

    Call, text, or email. If you have a question about your load, ask us.

  • Newer equipment.

    Late-model sleeper tractors and clean dry vans.

  • Updates when you need them.

    For booked loads, ask our operations team for current location and status.

Who we work with.

Shippers and freight brokers who need a truck to show up and a carrier that keeps them posted.

Shippers: send us the lane and the freight details. We'll tell you if we can cover it and what it will cost.

Brokers: our carrier details are below. If you need paperwork to get us set up, email us and we'll send it over.
